Astromind Explorer is a Flutter-based app packed with a variety of exciting mind games that will keep your brain active and entertained! 🌌🕹️

Explore a galaxy of challenges with:

🔢 Memory matching games

⏱️ Speed and focus tasks

🧩 Logic and pattern puzzles and more!

Each game is designed to help improve concentration, sharpen thinking skills, and, most of all, have fun while learning. Perfect for students, kids, and anyone who enjoys a good mental workout in a playful, space-themed world.
